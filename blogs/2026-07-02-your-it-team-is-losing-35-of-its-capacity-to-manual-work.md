SysAid's survey of 718 IT professionals found the average IT team loses 35% of its working time to manual, repetitive tasks, with nearly a quarter of respondents spending 40-60% of their day on such work. The post argues this gap between routine low-value work and strategic capacity is a structural productivity problem for IT managers, not just a statistic.
